Household Reminders

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Household Reminders is a private app used only by the members of one household. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

  • Google Tasks: the tasks in the "Household" list the app creates in a member's Google Tasks: titles, notes, due dates and whether they are completed. The app does not use any other task lists.
  • Email address: the Google account's email address, only to confirm that the right household member signed in.
  • Email: messages household members send or forward to the app's own address (for example a bill or appointment notice), to update the shared list, including the text of attached PDFs (read in memory on the household's computer, not stored). From forwarded emails the app keeps a short summary, the important links, the original sender and subject, and shipping tracking numbers. Full account and card numbers are not stored.

How it is used and stored

The dashboard is reached through Cloudflare, which carries the encrypted connection to the household's computer.

This information is used only to keep the household's shared to-do list and each member's "Household" task list in sync. It is stored on the household's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in a private file on that computer.

The app does not sell, share or transfer this information to anyone, does not show ads, and does not use it for any other purpose. Free text from emails may be processed by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) solely to turn a member's message into list changes.

Household Reminders' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Household calendar

The app keeps a shared "Household" calendar in its own Google account (not in members' accounts). It can only manage calendars it created and who they're shared with. It reads that calendar's upcoming events to show them in the morning email and on the dashboard.

Weather

To show the weather, the app sends the household's approximate location (city-level coordinates, no names or accounts) to Open-Meteo, a free weather service.

Removing access

A member can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting stops the sync; the member's "Household" task list stays in their Google account until they delete it.
