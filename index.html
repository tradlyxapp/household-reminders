Private household app

Household Reminders

What it is

Household Reminders is a small, private to-do list shared by the members of one household. It runs on the household's own computer and is not offered to the public.

It sends a morning email with open tasks, reads the household members' email replies to update the list, and keeps a "Household" list in each member's Google Tasks in sync, so tasks can be ticked off from Gmail, Google Calendar or the Google Tasks app.

Google account access

Household members connect their own Google account to let the app create, update and complete tasks in their "Household" task list. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is handled.
